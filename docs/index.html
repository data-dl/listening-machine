Four dashboards asked what you played. This one asks how you listen.

Every play row carries a timestamp, a skip flag, a shuffle flag, a country and a platform. Read across the full — plays — and, unlike every earlier build, converted from UTC to Eastern time first, so a 2am listen is a 2am listen — this is the shape of the listening rather than the list of it.

Playlists

Which of your playlists actually get played

How this is measured. The streaming history does not record which playlist a play came from — that field is blank in 97% of Spotify's own logs. So this counts plays of the tracks a playlist contains, wherever you played them. Pull-through, not usage.

Anomaly

Days that were not you

Someone else on the account leaves two marks at once: artists that appear on no other day of a fifteen-year record, and a device-country pair the account otherwise never uses. The pipeline flags any day of 50+ streams where at least 70% comes from artists heard on that day alone and the dominant device-country pair carries under 1% of lifetime streams — then excludes it from every figure on this page. A trip abroad on your own phone does not trip this; nor does an afternoon of rain-sound content farms on the home speaker.
